# FLATLINE — GAME DESIGN BIBLE
### Visual Identity, Character Art, UI Design & Asset Production Guide
**Document version 1.0 — Prepared for Google AI Studio asset generation + implementation**

---

## SECTION 1 — DESIGN PHILOSOPHY

### The Big Idea: "Emergency Ward Meets Bollywood"
Flatline lives in two visual worlds simultaneously:
**World 1 — The Clinical Dark:** ICU monitor. Black. Cold. Precise. EKG lines. Monospace numbers.
**World 2 — The Cartoon Chaos:** Vivid, loud, expressive Indian cartoon characters. Bold outlines, exaggerated proportions.

### Three design principles
1. **CONTRAST IS KING** — Dark everywhere, color only at maximum impact moments
2. **EVERYTHING HAS PERSONALITY** — No generic icons.
3. **DESI BUT NOT KITSCH** — Indian cultural elements used with taste.
